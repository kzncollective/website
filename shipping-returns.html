Shipping & Returns

Clear delivery and returns for small parts.

This is starter policy copy for launch planning. It should be finalised before taking payment.

UK shipping

Small parts are intended for tracked or standard small parcel dispatch from the UK. Final service levels and cut-off times can be added once fulfilment is set.

International shipping

International orders can be handled manually at launch if needed, especially where customs values and delivery estimates need checking.

Returns

Unused items can be returned within the stated returns window. Installed, modified or damaged items may need individual review.

Fitment mistakes

If you are unsure, ask before ordering. Product pages are designed to make model, year and fitment limits clear.
