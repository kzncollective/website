FAQ

Common questions before ordering.

Clear answers for fitment, guides, small batch products and launch checkout.

Is this only for Mk1 / NA MX-5s?

The launch range is focused on Mk1 / NA because those parts can be tested on an owned 1994 car. Other generations can be added once fitment is properly checked.

Why choose this over eBay or AliExpress?

The point is clarity: fitment notes, install guidance, owner testing, QC notes and support from someone building around the platform.

Will every product have an install guide?

The target is to provide guide links for anything that benefits from instructions, especially lighting, trim and gauge-related parts.

How will checkout work?

The static prototype can be wired to hosted checkout links first. If the store moves to WooCommerce, the design and product structure can be reused as the storefront direction.

Can I ask about fitment before buying?

Yes. Send model, year and photos if useful. It is better to confirm than order the wrong small part.
